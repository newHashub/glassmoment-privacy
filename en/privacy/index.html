Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 1, 2026

At a glance

GlassMoment requires no account, does not track users, includes no ads or analytics, and never uploads photos, compositions, or usage history to a developer server.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no account system, server-side database, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, third-party sign-in, or cloud sync.

Photo processing

Capture, import, glass effects, masks, text, previews, and export all run locally on your device. The app writes a result to the system Photos app only when you choose Save. If Save Original is enabled, the original photo is also written during that save.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used to capture photos and show the live viewfinder.
  • Add to Photos: Used to add results and optional originals that you choose to save.
  • Photo selection: Apple’s system picker lets you choose a photo to edit; the app can access only the items you select.

GlassMoment does not request microphone access.

Local History and Trash

After a save, the app keeps one local copy in its Application Support directory for Works/History, Favorites, and Trash. Moving a record to Trash is reversible. Permanent deletion and Empty Trash remove only the app’s local copy, never a photo in the system Photos app.

Preferences

GlassMoment uses UserDefaults to keep preferences such as camera ratio, recent glass style, onboarding status, and Save Original. These preferences stay on the device.

Deleting data

You can empty Trash or delete every in-app record from Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. These actions do not automatically delete photos already saved to the system library; delete those separately in Photos if desired.

Children and third parties

The app collects no user data, including data from children. This site uses no cookies, analytics, external fonts, or third-party scripts.
