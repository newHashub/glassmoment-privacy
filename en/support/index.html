How do I open Works and History?

Tap Works in the upper-left corner of the camera. Home shows every active record, the heart jar shows Favorites, and Trash contains recoverable soft-deleted records. The original right-swipe gesture remains available as a shortcut.

How do I favorite or trash an item?

In radial History, touch and hold an item without moving to favorite or unfavorite it. After the hold feedback appears, drag the item into Trash on the right. Home and Favorites also provide batch actions.

Does Trash delete photos from Apple Photos?

No. Move to Trash, permanent deletion, Empty Trash, and Delete All In-App Records affect only GlassMoment’s local copies. They never delete results or originals from the system Photos app.

The camera is unavailable

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and allow GlassMoment, or tap Open System Settings in About & Privacy. You can still use Apple’s photo picker to import an image when camera access is denied.

A photo will not save

Check that GlassMoment can add photos in the system Settings app. With Save Original off, one save adds one result to Photos. With it on, the result and original are both added, while Works still receives only one record.

Where is my data stored?

Composition preferences, Works, Favorites, and Trash are stored in the app container on this device. GlassMoment has no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app removes this local data but not photos in the system library.
